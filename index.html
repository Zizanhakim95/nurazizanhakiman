About

Business requirements in, working solutions out.

I joined Zen Computer Systems in 2022 as a pre-sales professional and now sit in the Solutions Architect Office, covering software solutions, technical proposal development, tender analysis and solution design.

My work spans system and application development, data analytics, business intelligence, data integration and digital solutions — built on technologies including Microsoft Power Platform, Power BI, SharePoint, relational databases, GIS and web applications. I support SME and enterprise projects end to end, from first discovery conversation to signed scope.

What makes the difference is the support background I came from: two years of root-cause diagnosis, SQL data verification and system testing before I ever wrote a proposal. Solutions I design are informed by how systems actually behave in production.

How I work

  1. Discover — workshops to map process, pain points and constraints.
  2. Translate — clear, testable software requirements.
  3. Design — scalable architecture aligned to budget and compliance.
  4. Propose — scope, timeline, cost and statement of work.
  5. Deliver — stay on the project as the client-side technical anchor.
